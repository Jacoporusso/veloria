## Final prompt

Create an original production game environment asset for Veloria, a modern illustrated 2D adventure for children. Reference image is STYLE ONLY: preserve its elegant hand-painted clear shapes and warm natural daylight, but design a different building. Subject: Ferraluce Energy Hall, a friendly contemporary sustainable energy laboratory, silvery brushed steel framing, ivory stone, broad blue-cyan glass, rooftop solar collectors and planted terraces, a restrained amber energy coil visible under a glass canopy. Not a train station; no trains, no turret or cathedral, no medieval wooden architecture. Slightly elevated orthographic view facing its front, 3:2 wide composition, isolated entire building and shallow foundation on transparent background. Building fills the image, centered with all edges visible and only a little padding. Front facade readable at mobile game scale, large planes and strong silhouette, few clean details, modern premium illustrated game art, no pixel art, no gritty industrial grime, no smoke. The lower frontage is a clean terrace behind a railing, not a traversable interior or prominent doorway. No characters, creatures, text, logos, labels, paths, roads, surrounding scenery or UI. Genuine alpha background, soft contact shadow only, no background rectangle. Intended to appear as a 24x16-tile landmark behind an outdoor plaza; collision and characters are separate game data.
