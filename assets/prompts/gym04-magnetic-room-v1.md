Create one production environment illustration asset for Veloria, a modern illustrated 2D children's exploration RPG, NOT pixel art, NOT retro, NOT a screenshot or UI mockup. A beautiful magnetic engineering gym interior platform seen in clean overhead 3/4 RPG perspective, north up, no camera rotation, square aligned floor and walls. Isolated on genuine transparent background. Entire room fully visible with breathing room, no cropping. A friendly sustainable technology atelier: brushed pale silver steel, pearl ceramic floor, turquoise glass, restrained lilac magnetic illumination, copper details, lush small potted greenery. Low rear wall with broad arched windows, a few large elegant induction coils at far rear corners, glass roof braces, soft daylight, rich painted detail and warm storytelling. Rectangular room, wide south entrance centered. Large unobstructed pale floor occupying at least 70 percent of interior so live game characters can walk and independently rendered conveyor strips/switches can be placed. NO characters, NO creatures, NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO logos, NO interface, NO grid, NO tiles, NO yellow border, NO decorative pathways or conveyors painted onto the central floor, NO isometric diamond floor. Floor is a level rectangular plane with horizontal front edge, vertical side edges, clear room silhouette. High quality hand-painted contemporary adventure game environment, organic details, legible silhouette, restrained lighting, soft contact shadows, polished rounded industrial design. This is one coherent room/platform asset, not a sprite sheet. Output landscape composition approximately 4:3.
