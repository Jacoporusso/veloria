## Final prompt

Original modern illustrated 2D adventure game environment ground asset, Veloria industrial garden district. A completely flat top-down orthographic continuous empty pale cool limestone/contemporary plaza ground surface, square image. Color family soft light blue-gray with a slight warm ivory balance. Very large quiet hand-painted color areas, extremely restrained low-frequency natural variation, premium clean child-friendly illustrated art. Mostly solid pale stone color, only a handful of broad subtly lighter patches; no small grain, no scratches, no speckling or repeated tiny texture, no bricks, no cobbles, no checkerboard, no tile grid, no distinct paving seams. This is the neutral background ground plane beneath separately authored paths, gardens, buildings and characters. Entire image filled edge to edge with only the continuous stone ground material; no objects, no plants, no shadows from objects, no walls, no borders, no road or path, no water, no landscape, no text or interface. Flat even daylight illumination, not a perspective scene, no vignette. It will cover a whole map once, not tile in a repeated pattern; avoid any directional features. Modern clean illustrated aesthetic rather than retro pixel art or photoreal macro texture.
