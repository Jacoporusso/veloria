## Prompt esatto

Create a polished original character art direction sheet for Veloria, a modern illustrated 2D exploration RPG for children. Landscape 3:2 board on warm off-white paper, very clean editorial layout, no text, no letters, no logos. Six distinct character columns, each with a large full-body three-quarter-front concept above and a small TOP-DOWN GAME SCALE version directly underneath. All characters share soft hand-painted 2D rendering, clear expressive faces, charming natural proportions with slightly enlarged heads for readability, clean broad shapes, subtle painted shadows, restrained material texture. NOT pixel art, NOT 3D render, NOT plastic dolls, NOT anime franchise copies. Columns left to right: (1) young adventurer child age 11 short dark chestnut hair, teal jacket, cream shirt, navy shorts, coral small backpack, practical sneakers; (2) another adventurer child age 11 brown skin curly hair in small puff, lavender jacket, cream shorts, mint backpack, practical sneakers; (3) older male botanical researcher silver curly hair and trimmed silver beard, round glasses, cream short lab coat over sage shirt, teal trousers, brown satchel; (4) adult female forest researcher dark skin short curly black hair, plum utility jacket, sand trousers, boots, small brass handheld scientific sensor; (5) adult female town shopkeeper warm tan skin long dark braid, coral apron over cream shirt, dark blue loose trousers, friendly confident face; (6) adult male railway attendant East Asian appearance short neat black hair, clean shaven, navy uniform with mint piping, cap, tiny lavender scarf. Distinct silhouette, age, hair, accessories and clothing for every role, no repeated faces. Bottom small versions must correspond exactly to their large counterpart, viewed from an elevated orthographic TOP DOWN camera appropriate for 2D map movement, feet visible, no ground scenery. Soft mint/coral/lavender accents match a warm natural village. Give generous whitespace and consistent character scale. This is a character design concept sheet, not an animation atlas.
